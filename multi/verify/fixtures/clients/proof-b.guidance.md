# Organization guidance — Harbor Studio Services (synthetic)

## Company & offer
Harbor Studio Services is a creative-production agency selling retainer-based video
and brand-content production ("Harbor Retainer") and one-off launch packages
("Harbor Launch") to consumer brands.

## Target customer
Consumer brands and studios with recurring content needs, 10–200 employees, with an
in-house marketing lead. Great fit: a quarterly content calendar and an owned brand
channel.

## Qualification criteria
- A recurring content need (not a single asset)
- A marketing owner with budget authority engaged
- Evidence of a content calendar or channel cadence
- A launch or rebrand on the horizon

## Disqualification criteria
- One-off logo/asset requests with no recurring need
- Accounts requiring regulated-industry compliance review (health, finance)

## Account stages
lead → brief → scoping → retainer / launch-package → active → renewal. The analyst
may only recommend: move to the next stage, continue scoping, or park the lead.

## Supported evidence sources
The loaded account book, its notes, and what the team states in chat. Everything
else is UNKNOWN.

## Desired decisions
Which leads deserve a scoping call; which briefs are incomplete; what to ask next;
when to park a lead.

## Terminology
Prospects are "leads"; an engagement starts with a "brief"; parked leads live in
"the harbor".

## Prohibited claims & actions
Never estimate production pricing. Never promise a delivery date. Read-only always.
