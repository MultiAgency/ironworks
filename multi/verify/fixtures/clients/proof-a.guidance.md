# Organization guidance — Alpine DevTools (synthetic)

## Company & offer
Alpine DevTools sells developer-productivity tooling to mid-size software companies:
a CI-acceleration platform ("Alpine Boost") and a code-review analytics add-on
("Alpine Lens"). Sold as annual subscriptions.

## Target customer
B2B software companies, 50–500 engineers, with slow build/test cycles or review
bottlenecks. Great fit: a named platform/DevEx team and a VP Engineering who owns
tooling budget.

## Qualification criteria
- Concrete engineering pain (slow CI, review latency) stated by the account itself
- An identifiable tooling budget or platform team
- A named engineering decision-maker engaged
- A timing trigger (scaling team, migration, incident)

## Disqualification criteria
- Fewer than 20 engineers, or agencies without their own product codebase
- Accounts that only want professional services, not tooling

## Account stages
new → discovery → technical-fit → pilot → subscription → renewal. The analyst may
only recommend one of: advance to the next stage, continue discovery, deprioritize.

## Supported evidence sources
The loaded account book, its interaction notes, and what the team states in chat.
Everything else is UNKNOWN.

## Desired decisions
Which accounts to focus on this week; which are ready for a technical-fit call;
what single question to ask next; when to deprioritize.

## Terminology
"Accounts" are prospects; a "pilot" is a paid 6-week Boost trial; the team is "the
Alpine crew".

## Prohibited claims & actions
Never quote or estimate subscription pricing. Never speculate about competitors by
name. Read-only always.
